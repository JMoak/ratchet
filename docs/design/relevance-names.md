# Names for the relevance application

Naming study, 2026-10-20. These are proposals for the application described in
[subject-relevance.md](subject-relevance.md), not a decision to rename Ratchet.

**Preferred direction: Germane.** It names the central relationship: useful material
for a particular subject, question, and person. It is seven letters, two syllables
("jer-MAYN"), and makes no claim that relevance establishes truth. Use the plain
name, with **"a research workspace built on Ratchet"** as an explanatory line.
The existing Germane Software business makes this a creative recommendation pending
further name review, not a launch-ready availability claim.

## The three to discuss

| Name | Why it fits | Main reservation | Tagline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Germane** | Precise and restrained. Fits subject discovery, goal-specific evidence, and project relevance equally well. The application supplies material that bears on the question; Ratchet supplies the decision discipline. | Slightly formal; some people will hear the given name Jermaine. Existing software use needs investigation. | **Research that bears on your question.** |
| **Reading Field** | Calm, open, and editorial. A field can be explored before there is a precise goal, then narrowed to the material needed for a project. Both words are familiar and easy to say. | Less compact and distinctive. Could sound like a reading education service, and foregrounds text over other sources. | **Explore a subject. Read with a purpose.** |
| **Bearings** | The most memorable metaphor: get oriented, see several directions, and choose a route. Its mechanical meaning gives Ratchet a subtle family resemblance while "has a bearing on" suggests relevance. | Crowded search territory, an exact AI research name, and a near match to Bearing AI. Retain as a tonal reference, with substantial collision concerns. | **Find your way through a subject.** |

Sentences to say aloud:

- "I gave **Germane** the approval-design papers and a twenty-minute reading budget;
  its pack kept the failure cases beside the supporting evidence."
- "Open **Reading Field** and make a beginner's pack from the same sources we used
  for the implementation review."
- "**Bearings** showed me the questions in this collection before I chose which one
  to investigate."

Germane is the precise research direction; Reading Field is the welcoming editorial
direction; Bearings is the exploratory direction. Reading Field is the strongest
alternative if the existing uses make Germane impractical.

## Preliminary collision screen

Checked public web results on 2026-10-20, using exact names with terms including
`app`, `software`, `AI`, and `research`. This is an early product-name screen,
**not trademark or domain clearance**. No domain availability, registrations,
ownership rights, or international name clearance were established. Search absence
is not evidence of availability.

- **Germane:** [Germane Software](https://germane-software.com/en/index.html) describes
  software products and consulting. The page is reachable; its present business
  activity was not established. This is a real software naming concern, although the
  examined page does not describe a competing research workspace.
- **Reading Field:** `"Reading Field" software` and `"Reading Field" research app`
  did not surface an obvious exact-name software or AI research product in the
  returned results. Results did include ordinary descriptive uses in reading and
  technical literature. Treat this as **unresolved**, not cleared.
- **Bearings:** [Bearing AI](https://www.bearing.ai/) markets maritime intelligence;
  [Bearings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23152) is also the exact name of a
  self-supervised audio research framework. These create search and identity
  confusion even though their tasks differ.
- **Afield:** [Afield](https://afield.lol/) describes an AI workflow beginning with
  market research and continuing into campaign execution.
- **Footing:** [Footing](https://www.footing.tech/) offers Business Central onboarding,
  while [another Footing](https://footingtool.com/) offers construction reporting.
- **Foreland:** [ForeLand AI](https://foreland.ai/) describes a property-development
  assistant; [Foreland](https://about.foreland.io/) is a prediction platform.
- **Offprint:** [Offprint](https://www.readoffprint.com/) explicitly offers research
  discovery and collections. This is the closest category collision.
- **Threadmark:** [Threadmark's extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/threadmark/epcicmdladhpnbmgfgbokfnapilbhpej)
  saves and organizes AI conversation passages; a separate
  [Threadmark repository](https://github.com/thinkwright/threadmark) provides continuity
  between coding assistants.
- **Sourcefold:** [SourceFold](https://github.com/0xAungkon/SourceFold) packages code
  into a single file for AI context.
- **Selvedge:** [Selvedge](https://selvedge.sh/) is decision memory for coding agents,
  very close to Ratchet's surrounding vocabulary.

## Keep the product boundary legible

Ratchet remains the decision engine. The application name belongs on the research
workspace, subject views, and evidence packs. Prefer "**Germane, built on Ratchet**"
in technical introductions; everyday interface copy can simply say "Germane."
Avoid "Ratchet Research" or "Ratchet AI" as the primary app name: either would make
an application-specific workflow sound like the engine's full purpose.

Use ordinary feature names such as **Subjects**, **Questions**, **Sources**, and
**Evidence packs**. Name the benefit as helping someone investigate a question;
leave claims of completeness, correctness, or measured precision to the evidence.
