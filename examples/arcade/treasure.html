    How this world works

    Each expedition is 300 digs. Scout's true accuracy is one of 86%, 91%, 94%, 97%, 99% and 99.7%, which the crew cannot see, and in some worlds it slips ten points partway through. While Scout is supervised you dig and every proposal is labelled; once promoted, only audited digs are.

    The first arm looks after every dig and promotes when the last twenty read at the promise. The second is the engine's rollout controller: it fixes the sample before any label is read, evaluates it once by an exact bound, rejects a Scout that fails it, and after promotion reads only the audits. A Scout sent back has to pass a fresh sample.

    What to look for: looking every time promotes nearly every Scout, the planned test pays for its caution by passing only about half the good ones, and the three demotion rules trade empty pits nobody saw against good Scouts sent back for nothing.
