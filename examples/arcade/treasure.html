What the engine is learning

  1. Record all 12 practice judgments in a real ledger, then attach the toy world's labels.
  2. Fit an exploratory threshold only from those unselected shadow rows.
  3. Keep the expedition separate. Its outcomes never enter the fit, even after reveal.

The bold compass changes its claims while the buried treasure stays fixed. More confidence cannot create more evidence. The suggested threshold targets an empirical 80% treasure share with at least three practice examples; it is not a statistical guarantee.
