THE IDEA UNDER THE GAME

Keep verified progress.

Each proposal goes through a real Ratchet gate. Only an actual act places a piece. Review and refusal leave the crossing unchanged.

The load test supplies a label after the judgment. Future decisions see the built bridge and the next load, never previous confidence scores.

This is a small simulated world, not a structural engineering model or a production accuracy claim. A real load rule this simple belongs in deterministic code; here it gives us known truth for teaching an imperfect verifier.
